## Personal Projects

### REDESIGNFIELDS

**Rural Land Intelligence & Regenerative Development**<br>
*2026–Present · Navarra · Personal Project / Concept Development*

REDESIGNFIELDS is a concept-stage rural land intelligence and regenerative development project based around a
4,300 m² pilot parcel in Navarra connected to a personal rural-land investment. It brings together rural land
assessment, GIS, cadastral boundaries and parcel analysis to examine access, land-use constraints, topography,
water, soil, climate and environmental factors alongside economic feasibility.

The intended workflow is:

**Parcel diagnosis → concept design → permitting guidance → infrastructure planning → implementation → long-term monitoring**

The project is currently at the concept and presentation-development stage. It is connected to an approximately
€30k personal investment in rural land for segregation and the development of regenerative and educational
productive-garden uses.
