Professional Profile

My professional profile combines business development, project management, technology and industrial implementation. I work across the development of industrial and technology projects, connecting market needs, commercial opportunities and operational delivery.

Since 2025, I have worked at ENVIROHEMP in Navarra as Business Development and Projects Management (Deputy Manager). My work covers biochar, activated carbon and circular-economy technologies, including market analysis, technology scouting, customer validation, business-case development, commercial negotiation and partnership development.

I contribute to modular and containerised industrial systems from supplier coordination and product development through commissioning, scale-up and industrial deployment. This also involves R&D project management, including European projects, regulatory processes and the operational implementation of decarbonisation and circular-economy technologies.

My interests also include rural land assessment and regenerative land development, using GIS, cadastral and parcel analysis, spatial data and site constraints to assess access, permitting considerations and project feasibility.
